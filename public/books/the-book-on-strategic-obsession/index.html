The Book On Strategic Obsession

How to Turn Long-Term Thinking Into a Competitive Weapon

By David Webb

Most people think in days, weeks, or months. The few who believe in decades—and align every action to that horizon—change the game entirely. The Book On Strategic Obsession is about becoming one of those few.

This is not a celebration of overwork or blind persistence. It’s about building a sustained, disciplined focus on a long-term objective, structuring your life and work so that every decision compounds toward it. Strategic obsession means clarity of aim, ruthless prioritization, and an ability to ignore the noise that pulls others off course.

Inside, you’ll learn:

  • How to define a long-term objective that is both ambitious and achievable.
  • The architecture of focus that keeps you locked on target over years, not weeks.
  • How to engineer feedback loops that refine your path without breaking your momentum.
  • The role of patience as a competitive advantage.
  • How to resist the lure of short-term wins that sabotage the bigger play.

Drawing from strategic planning, behavioral science, and case studies of leaders who have achieved seemingly impossible goals, this book gives you the frameworks to outlast and outperform competitors who burn out or drift.

The result isn’t just achieving a goal—it’s building an identity as the kind of operator who stays the course when others can’t.

If you’ve ever wondered why some people or organizations seem unstoppable, this is the manual for understanding—and becoming—one of them.
