The Book On AI for Everyday People

by Alex Hartman

Artificial intelligence isn’t just in research labs or Hollywood blockbusters—it’s in your pocket, your workplace, your shopping cart, and your newsfeed. AI for Everyday People is the plain-English guide to understanding how AI works, where it’s used, and how it’s shaping the world you live in.

Written for non-technical readers, this book strips away the jargon and hype to explain AI in a way that’s clear, engaging, and practical. You’ll learn:

  • What AI is—and isn’t—in terms anyone can understand.
  • How AI powers everyday tools like voice assistants, recommendation systems, and fraud detection.
  • The ethical questions and societal challenges AI raises.
  • How AI is transforming healthcare, education, government, and business.
  • What the future of work looks like in an AI-driven economy.
  • How to spot misinformation and think critically about AI’s role in society.

Through relatable examples and real-world case studies, AI for Everyday People gives you the literacy to join the conversation about one of the most important technologies of our time. Whether you’re curious, cautious, or excited about AI, this book will help you navigate the opportunities and risks ahead.
