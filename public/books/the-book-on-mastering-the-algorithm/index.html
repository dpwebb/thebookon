The Book On Mastering the Algorithm

The Book On Mastering the Algorithm: How to Go Viral, Stay Visible, and Thrive in the Age of Attention

By Rowan Blake

In the economy of attention, visibility is everything. But today’s algorithms decide who gets seen, who gets heard, and who fades into digital obscurity. Mastering the Algorithm is your field guide to building lasting visibility and influence in a world where feeds shape reality.

This is not about chasing trends or gaming systems for short-term spikes. It’s about understanding the mechanics of attention—how algorithms track behavior, amplify signals, and reward consistency—and using that knowledge to build a presence that compounds over time.

You’ll learn how to:

  • Identify the unique signal you want the algorithm to recognize.
  • Create content patterns that train the system to surface your work.
  • Build trust and engagement that outlasts platform changes.
  • Avoid burnout by focusing on sustainable visibility strategies.
  • Turn attention into lasting opportunity without sacrificing integrity.

Grounded in systems thinking, this book helps creators, entrepreneurs, and professionals stop relying on luck and start building strategic gravity online.
