The Book On Operational Excellence

From High-Level Strategy to Ground-Level Execution

By Julian Mercer

Operational excellence isn’t a buzzword. It’s the difference between organizations that consistently deliver and those that slowly break down under their weight. The Book On Operational Excellence strips the concept to its essentials and rebuilds it as a practical, repeatable discipline.

This is not theory for theory’s sake. It’s a hands-on framework for designing, executing, and sustaining high-performance operations in any environment. You’ll learn how to align strategy with execution, eliminate friction from workflows, and embed resilience so you can adapt without losing momentum.

Inside, you’ll find:

The architecture of world-class operations, from core processes to cultural foundations.

How to run a “friction audit” to identify and remove hidden performance killers.

Scalable systems that prevent dependency on single points of failure.

Ways to balance efficiency with adaptability in fast-changing markets.

Recovery and redundancy strategies that keep you running when things go wrong.

This book draws on decades of operational leadership, process optimization, and organizational design to provide you with actionable tools that you can deploy immediately. It’s written for leaders, operators, and builders who understand that remarkable results aren’t an accident—they’re engineered.

If you want your operation—whether a startup, a global division, or a high-stakes project—to perform at the highest level, The Book On Operational Excellence hands you the blueprint.
