The Book On Relationships: How We Connect, Why We Hurt, and What Love Can Become

By C.S. Morgan

Love is more than chemistry, more than a feeling—it’s a practice, shaped by our histories, our habits, and the ways we choose to show up. Relationships is a deep, compassionate exploration of how we connect, why we sometimes hurt each other, and how love can grow into something more prosperous and more resilient.

Blending psychology, personal reflection, and practical guidance, C.S. Morgan takes you through the patterns that shape relationships—from attachment styles and communication breakdowns to conflict repair and long-term intimacy. You’ll explore:

How childhood experiences influence adult connection.

The hidden scripts that drive attraction and conflict.

How to set and respect boundaries without losing closeness.

The role of vulnerability, trust, and friendship in lasting love.

Navigating digital-age challenges like swipe culture and ghosting.

Rebuilding after breakups or breaches of trust.

Every chapter includes reflections to help you apply insights to your relationships—romantic, platonic, or familial. The goal isn’t perfect love; it’s a more honest, intentional connection.
